1. Ordering and pickup

  • Your order is confirmed when payment is accepted and you see your order number. We'll start making it for your chosen time.
  • As soon as possible orders are taken during open hours, until 15 minutes before closing. Scheduled orders can be placed up to 7 days ahead for any open time we offer.
  • Drive-up: tap "I'm here" on your order page when you arrive, pick your parking spot, and we'll bring it out. Please give the details we ask for (name, spot, car) so we can find you.
  • Please pick up at your chosen time. Food held more than 60 minutes after it's ready may be thrown out for food safety, and we can't refund orders that aren't picked up.
  • Menu items and prices can change, and items can sell out. If something you ordered isn't available, we'll offer a swap or a refund for that item.

2. Prices, tax and fees

  • Prices are in U.S. dollars. Ohio sales tax (currently 6.75% here) is added at checkout.
  • Website orders include an online processing fee of 3.3% + $0.30, shown before you pay. Drive-up and kiosk orders placed at the truck have no processing fee.
  • Tips are optional and go to our team.
  • If a price is shown by mistake, we may cancel the order and refund you in full, or contact you to confirm the correct price.

3. Payment

  • Card, Apple Pay and Google Pay payments are processed by Square. We never see or store your full card number.
  • Kiosk cash orders are paid at the window before you receive your food.
  • If a payment is declined or reversed, we may cancel the order.

4. Changes, cancellations and refunds

  • Need to change or cancel? Call us at (330) 587-5203 as soon as possible. Before we start cooking, we can usually cancel with a full refund. Once your food is made, we generally can't.
  • Something wrong with your order? Tell us at pickup, or within 24 hours with your order number, and we'll make it right with a remake, a credit or a refund.
  • If we have to cancel your order (for example, the truck can't open), you'll get a full refund.
  • Refunds go back to your original payment method. Your bank may take 5–10 business days to show it. Points from a refunded order are removed, as explained in the Rewards terms.
  • The online processing fee is refunded when we cancel your order or refund it in full.

5. Hours, events and pauses

  • Our hours, holiday closures and special hours are posted on the website and can change. Online ordering follows them automatically.
  • When the truck is at an event or private catering, or the kitchen is very busy, we may pause online ordering. Paused times can't be ordered.
  • Weather, mechanical or safety issues can make us close early. If that affects your order, we'll contact you and refund what we can't serve.

  • Promo codes and offers have no cash value, can't be combined unless we say so, and may have limits, dates and minimum orders.

Our free loyalty program has its own terms, which you agree to when you join. They're shown on the Rewards section of our website. In short:

  • Earn 10 points for each $1 spent on food. Tax, tips, fees and gift cards don't earn points.
  • Redeemed rewards are final. Points have no cash value and can't be transferred.
  • Points from canceled or refunded orders are removed.
  • Platinum: members who have earned 900 points in total unlock our secret menu while it's offered. Points you spend still count toward Platinum.
  • We may change or end the program and will post changes on the website.

8. Gift cards

  • "Take it off" choices (like no onions) are made by request, but trace amounts may remain.
