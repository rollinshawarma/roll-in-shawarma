Roll-In Shawarma started with a simple bet: Ohio was ready for shawarma carved and rolled fresh, not steamed in a hotel pan.

The truck itself is a rebuilt 2004 Freightliner, gutted and outfitted with a brand-new kitchen setup from the frame up, licensed and inspected through Summit County Public Health. Every recipe on the menu was built and priced to hold up at scale.

We're a small, hands-on crew — the kind of truck where the person handing you your food is the same person who built the menu.
