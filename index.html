We Roll It. You Love It.

Roll-In Shawarma started with a simple bet: Ohio was ready for shawarma carved and rolled fresh, not steamed in a hotel pan.

The truck itself is a rebuilt 2004 Freightliner, gutted and outfitted from the frame up, licensed and inspected through Summit County Public Health. Every recipe on the menu, from the frankie rolls to the loaded fries, was built and priced to hold up at scale.

We're a small, hands-on crew — the kind of truck where the person handing you your food is the same person who built the menu.
